About Fragrance House

We are passionate about bringing you the finest organic fragrances sourced from the heart of the Middle East and crafted right here in Pakistan. Every bottle tells a story of heritage, purity, and craftsmanship.

Founded over a decade ago in Lahore, our mission has always been simple: to make premium, 100% natural attars and perfume oils accessible to everyone. We believe that luxury fragrance should not come at a luxury price.

Our master perfumers combine traditional distillation methods passed down through generations with modern quality standards to create fragrances that are both authentic and long-lasting.

Our Values
