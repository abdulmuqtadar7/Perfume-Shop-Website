Notre Histoire

A house built on patience.

Maison Velour began with a single bottle, a single accord, and an unreasonable obsession with getting one scent exactly right. Eleven years later, we're still doing the same thing — just twelve times over.

Founded in 2014 by perfumer Héloïse Marchand above the flower fields of Grasse, Maison Velour makes fragrance the way it was made a century ago — slowly, by hand, from botanicals we've known growers for a decade. Every drop is macerated for a minimum of nine months before it ever touches glass.

Our Values

Four things we refuse to compromise.

01

Raw materials

We use rare naturals — Tuscan iris, Bulgarian rose, Laotian oud — sourced directly from growers we know by name.

02

Slow maceration

Nine months minimum, often twelve. Patience is what lets the notes marry. There is no shortcut to softness.

03

Small batches

Every batch is 300 bottles, signed and numbered. If it sells out, it sells out. We will not mass-produce.

04

Refill, reuse

Send any bottle back for a 20% refill credit. The glass is designed to last a lifetime; the scent inside to change with you.

The Perfumer

Héloïse Marchand, Nose.

Trained at the Grasse Institute and at Givaudan, Héloïse founded Maison Velour after ten years creating scents for houses she'd rather not name. "I wanted to make one bottle I was proud of," she says. "Then I made twelve."

She still hand-signs the first bottle of every new batch.
