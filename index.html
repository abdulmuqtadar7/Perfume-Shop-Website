The Maison

A decade in a single drop.

Maison Velour was founded in 2014 in a quiet atelier above the flower fields of Grasse. Every fragrance is macerated for at least nine months, blended in micro-batches, and hand-filled into apothecary glass. We never compromise on the ingredients, and we never will.
