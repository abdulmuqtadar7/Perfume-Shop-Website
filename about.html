Our Story

Parc de Faris was born from a deep love of traditional Arabic and French perfumery — a desire to bring the most authentic, natural, and luxurious fragrances to Pakistan. Our founder, inspired by the great perfume houses of Grasse and the ancient oud souks of Arabia, spent years sourcing the finest raw materials from across the world.

Every bottle of Parc de Faris is a story — of rare oud harvested from the forests of Assam, of roses hand-picked at dawn in Taif, of saffron threads from the fields of Kashmir. We believe that fragrance is not just a scent, but an experience, a memory, an emotion.

Today, Parc de Faris serves thousands of fragrance lovers across Pakistan, offering an exclusive range of pure attars, oud blends, French-inspired perfumes, and traditional bakhoor — all crafted with uncompromising attention to quality and authenticity.
