Our maison

A quiet house, for louder memories.

Maison Noir began in a narrow atelier above a jasmine field. Fifty years later we still bottle by hand, in runs of a few hundred. We don't chase trends — we chase the twelve seconds a scent lingers after a person leaves a room.

03
